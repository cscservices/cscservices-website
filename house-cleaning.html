The scope of the job

01

Living areas

  • Sweeping, mopping and vacuuming of floors and carpets
  • Dusting of furniture, shelves and surfaces
  • Tidying and general arranging of each room
02

Kitchen & bathroom

  • Wipe-down of counters, stovetop and cabinet fronts
  • Sinks, toilets, tubs and bathroom fixtures cleaned and disinfected
  • Rubbish bins emptied and liners replaced

Scheduling
