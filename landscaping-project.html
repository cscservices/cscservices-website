Big jobs, handled start to finish

From hazardous trees to large clean-ups, we bring the people, the transport and the bins.

  • Dead or hazardous trees removed following safety protocols
  • Trimming for clearance from buildings, pathways and power lines
  • Lorry transportation for materials, plants or debris
  • RORO bins for large-scale waste collection
  • Bed line trenching and edging around planting beds
  • Irrigation inspection and repair

How We Work

Safe, tidy and kind to the site

Trained operators

Machinery is only operated by trained and authorised crew, in uniforms supplied by CSC Services.

Full PPE

Gloves, safety boots and eye and ear protection whenever mowing, trimming or using a blower.

Safe chemical handling

Fertilisers and pesticides are applied by label instructions and SDS guidelines, never before heavy rain.

Environmental care

No soil erosion or water run-off, and organic waste is composted or sent for green-waste disposal.
